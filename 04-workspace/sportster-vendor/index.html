Privacy Policy
Last updated: 15 January 2025 · Effective immediately
1. Information We Collect
We collect information you provide during registration (name, CNIC, email, phone, business details) and operational data such as arena listings, bookings, pricing, and transaction records. Usage data including app interactions may also be collected.
2. How We Use Your Information
Your data is used to operate the Sportster platform, process payouts, verify vendor identity, resolve disputes, improve our services, and send relevant notifications. We do not sell personal data to third parties.
3. Data Security
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols. Access to vendor data is restricted to authorized Sportster personnel only.
4. Your Rights
You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting support@sportster.com. Account deletion removes all personal data within 30 days.
5. Contact Us
For privacy-related queries, email privacy@sportster.com or write to Sportster Technologies, DHA Phase 2, Karachi, Pakistan.

Terms & Conditions
Vendor Terms · Last updated: 15 January 2025
1. Vendor Eligibility
To list arenas on Sportster, you must be a legal owner or authorized operator of the facility, provide accurate documents during registration, and maintain a valid business status. Sportster reserves the right to reject or remove any listing.
2. Arena Listings
All arena information must be accurate and up-to-date. Misleading descriptions, false amenities, or inflated capacity are grounds for immediate suspension. Vendors must honour all confirmed bookings.
3. Payments & Commissions
Sportster charges a platform commission of 10–15% per booking. Remaining earnings are disbursed weekly to the registered bank account. Sportster may adjust commission rates with 30-day advance notice.
4. Cancellations
Vendors must set a clear cancellation policy. Cancellations initiated by the vendor after customer payment will result in a full refund to the customer and a cancellation penalty deducted from the vendor's next payout.
5. Termination
Sportster may suspend or permanently remove a vendor account for repeated policy violations, fraudulent activity, or failure to honour bookings. Vendors may close their account by contacting support with 14-days notice.
Questions about these terms? Email legal@sportster.com
