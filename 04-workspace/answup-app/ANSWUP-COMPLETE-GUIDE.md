# ANSWUP — THE COMPLETE GUIDE
How the whole business works, both sides. Plain language. (Vendor-only detail lives in VENDOR-PLAYBOOK.md.)

## THE ONE-PARAGRAPH VERSION
A tradesman finds answup.com, plays the demo, signs up through a friendly chat with Ava. His AI receptionist builds itself instantly. He test-calls it, loves it, pays you. You give him a phone number, he forwards his line to it, and from then on his AI answers every missed call, books the job, and texts him the lead. You run everything from one admin screen. He pays $397–$1,197 every month.

---

## PART 1 — WHAT THE CUSTOMER DOES (5 easy touchpoints)
1. **Discovers answup.com** — the ringing-iPhone demo sells him. Clicks Get Started, picks a plan, signs in with Google.
2. **Chats with Ava** — one question at a time (business name, trade, area, hours, emergencies, where to text leads). Taps options or types, skips anything. Feels like texting. ~1 minute.
3. **Test-calls his own AI** — "📞 Call your AI right now" button on his dashboard; talks to it in the browser. The moment he's sold.
4. **Clicks Activate & pays** — invoice appears, he pays by bank transfer.
5. **Forwards his phone** — dashboard shows his number, a Copy button, and the exact dial code for his carrier. Dials it once. Done.

After that he lives in his texts, not the dashboard. Leads arrive by SMS. He opens the dashboard occasionally to see the monthly scoreboard (calls, leads, minutes) that proves the value.

## PART 2 — WHAT YOU (THE VENDOR) DO — four actions per client
1. **Read their chat** (admin Inbox) — refine the auto-built AI if needed.
2. **Tick "Paid"** in their drawer when the transfer lands — unlocks go-live.
3. **Buy their Twilio number** (~$1.15/mo), paste into their drawer.
4. **Click "Set Live"** — their dashboard flips to forwarding steps; they forward; they're live.

Daily: 5 min in admin Inbox + a glance at All Calls. Monthly: send invoices, tick paid.

## PART 3 — WHAT RUNS WITH ZERO TOUCH
- Signup → auto-accepted, kickoff questions auto-sent
- Onboarding done → AI builds itself (Vapi)
- Client clicks Activate → invoice auto-created
- Every real call → answered, transcribed, scored, saved to both dashboards, and (once Twilio SMS is on) the owner gets a text with the lead in seconds

## PART 4 — YOUR THREE ADMIN SCREENS
- **Clients** — pipeline board (New → Building → Live → Paused → Rejected). Each card shows the one next thing to do. Click for the drawer: details, Paid checkbox, invoice button, "Build their AI" button, phone-number field, chat, Delete.
- **Inbox** — every client conversation. Daily check.
- **All Calls** — every call across all clients: names, transcripts, recordings.

## PART 5 — THE MONEY
- Per client: ~30 min of your time + ~$0.12/min call + $1.15/mo number + a few cents/text, vs. $397–$1,197/mo revenue.
- Target: 5–10 clients before August = $4,500–$7,000+/mo. One or two signups a week gets there.
