User is looking to buy a MacBook for AI and robotics work.

**Budget discussed:** Started at 1 lakh PKR, stretched to 1.5 lakh, open to more.

- M-series chip only (no Intel)
- Used/refurbished market in Pakistan (OLX, Facebook Marketplace)

**Sweet spots identified:**

- Best value (3.5–4 lakh): Pro M2 Pro 14" 32GB ← top pick

**Why:** User works in AI + robotics, needs sustained performance, local ML model inference, and ROS simulations.
